# Reporting misuse or a safety problem

Please do not open a public issue for misuse of the model, for suspected illegal content, or for a safety problem with
the weights, such as a way to make the model draw a minor. Email **abuse@verboa.io** instead.

Describe the file you used, the prompt and the settings. Do not attach or link any image that may show a minor.

For bugs and loading problems, open an issue. For anything else, email contact@verboa.io.
